01 THE STUDIO

Thoughtful design.
Beautifully executed.

BLOVEN DECOR is an event styling and design studio based in Kampala, Uganda, creating considered environments for weddings, celebrations, corporate occasions and meaningful gatherings.

We believe the most memorable events are not defined simply by how much is placed in a room, but by how everything comes together.

From the first idea to the final detail, we approach every project with an eye for balance, atmosphere, proportion and personality.

02 OUR PHILOSOPHY

Less decoration.
More intention.

01

Intention

Every element has a reason to be there. We design with purpose, ensuring that colour, texture, scale and placement contribute to the experience rather than simply filling the space.

02

Balance

We are drawn to spaces that feel effortless. Our styling considers contrast, proportion, movement and negative space to create environments that feel complete without feeling crowded.

03

Character

Your event should feel like yours. We take inspiration from the people, stories and occasions behind every project and translate them into a visual experience with its own identity.

03 OUR APPROACH

From first thought to final detail.

01

Discover

We begin by understanding your event, your vision, your audience and the feeling you want the space to create.

02

Develop

Ideas become a visual direction. We consider colour, materials, florals, furniture, lighting, tablescapes and spatial flow.

03

Refine

The smallest details often make the greatest difference. We refine every element until the concept feels cohesive and distinctly yours.

04

Create

On the day, our work comes to life. We transform the plan into an environment designed to be experienced, photographed and remembered.
